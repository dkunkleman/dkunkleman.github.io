# Guided Field Instructions

1. Open the HTTPS `/field/` app before leaving service and wait for **Offline ready**.
2. Tap **Start Inspection**. The app starts GPS, preserves the start record, retrieves or reuses authoritative weather, and opens the default Arrival mission.
3. Follow the current mission. Use **Start Capture** for the expected feature or any large field button for an unexpected feature. The mission stays active.
4. For each feature: **Mark it, Measure it, Photograph it, Explain it, Connect it**. Use Unknown or Unsafe rather than guessing.
5. Tap **Save Minimum Record** when essential fields are complete. A measured depth requires value, unit, method and basis. Soil penetration stays locked until both acknowledgments are recorded.
6. Use **Mark Route Condition** whenever access changes. Leave that Feature Capture Session active while traversing the segment; its walked GPS line and exact point references are saved until you complete or defer the segment. Record mode, width, clearance, grade, surface, wetness, cross-slope, and passability. Keep GPS running while jumping between missions.
7. Use mission progress to see incomplete sessions, unassigned photographs, measurement gaps and unknown areas. A nearby GPS track does not prove the ground was inspected.
8. Tap **Finish Inspection**. Resolve each issue, mark it Unknown, skip with a reason, or accept the incomplete record honestly.
9. Save the single ChatGPT Analysis Package. Do not press Clear until it is safely stored in the Property Intelligence Repository.

Safety controls collection. Never cross water, stand in traffic, enter unsafe brush, leave authorized property, or penetrate soil without authorization and underground-utility safety acknowledgment merely to complete a prompt.
